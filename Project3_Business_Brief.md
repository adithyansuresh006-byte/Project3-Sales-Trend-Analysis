# E-commerce Sales Trend Analysis
**Project 3 — Data Science & AI Internship**

## Context
This project analyses 2,000 simulated e-commerce orders from 2025 to identify revenue trends, product category performance, and valuable customers.

## Key Findings
- **Best revenue month:** February 2025.
- **Lowest revenue month:** August 2025.
- **Top category:** Fashion, with revenue of 853,984.93 across 700 orders.
- **Customer contribution:** The top 10% of customers contributed 18.94% of total revenue.

## Recommendations
1. **Focus on Fashion:** Maintain popular fashion products in stock and use targeted promotions.
2. **Improve customer loyalty:** Introduce rewards and personalized offers to encourage repeat purchases.
3. **Improve low-revenue periods:** Investigate August's performance and consider promotions during weaker months.

## Next Steps
Monitor monthly revenue, category performance, and customer spending. Evaluate the effectiveness of promotions and update the analysis as new order data becomes available.

## Data Note
The project uses generated, simulated e-commerce data. Findings are illustrative and do not represent actual customers or business transactions.
